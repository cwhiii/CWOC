📜 What is CWOC?

A self-hosted task, note, and calendar management app built around one flexible record — the chit. A chit can be a task, note, calendar event, alarm, checklist, or project, all in one unified data model.

Unlike most productivity tools, CWOC doesn't force you to pick a category up front. Fill in the fields that matter and the system figures out where it belongs. A chit with a date shows up on the calendar. Add a checklist and it appears in checklists too. Tag it and it filters with everything else.

Available as a web app and a native Android app. Learn more & download →

No subscriptions. No cloud dependency. Your data lives on your hardware in a single SQLite file, accessible from any device on your network.

💡 Philosophy

  • One record, infinite uses. A chit adapts to what you need — no rigid categories, no separate apps for separate things.
  • Your data, your hardware. Self-hosted, single SQLite file, no cloud lock-in. You own everything.
  • No build step, no framework. Vanilla JS, HTML, CSS. Lightweight enough to run on a Raspberry Pi.
  • Connections surface naturally. When everything lives on the same record, every field makes every view smarter.
